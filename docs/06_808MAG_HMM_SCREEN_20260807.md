# Complete 808-MAG AioA/IdrA HMM screen

## Input quality control

- 808 non-empty dereplicated MAG protein FASTA files.
- 1,893,843 proteins after concatenation.
- MAG-prefixed target identifiers contained no duplicates.
- The previous 129,785-protein database represented the 48 known
  K08356-bearing MAGs, not the complete 808-MAG collection.

## Model provenance

Models were taken from the first-author repository associated with
Reyes-Umana et al.:

<https://github.com/vmreyes12/Neighborhood_Analyses/tree/main/hmm>

The repository README explicitly demonstrates a 640 score threshold with
`combined_iriA_aioA.hmm`. The same numerical threshold was applied separately
to `iriA_new.hmm` and `aioA.hmm` only as exploratory sensitivity/comparator
screens because model-specific calibrated thresholds are not documented there.

## Screen results

| Screen | Hits | Unique MAGs | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Combined AioA/IdrA T640 | 50 | 49 | Primary discovery screen |
| IdrA-specific T640 | 55 | 54 | Exploratory sensitivity screen |
| AioA-specific T640 | 3 | 3 | Exploratory comparator |

Comparison with the 48 existing K08356 candidates produced ten union-level new
targets:

- two passed the primary combined-model T640 screen;
- eight additional targets passed only the exploratory IdrA-specific T640
  screen;
- the ten proteins were 418-675 aa long, shorter than many complete IdrA/AioA
  catalytic subunits and therefore requiring explicit truncation/domain checks.

The two primary-screen additions were:

| MAG | Protein | Combined score | IdrA-specific score | Length (aa) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `SY457BB-0-4_bin10` | `SY457BB-0-4_bin10-k141_729355_7` | 670.6 | 1021.8 | 675 |
| `SQ_81_0-4_bin5` | `SQ_81_0-4_bin5-k141_112333_1` | 643.8 | 903.0 | 547 |

## Interpretation boundary

These ten proteins are additional HMM candidates, not ten confirmed IdrA
proteins. Final classification requires placement in the AioA/IdrA/other-DMSOR
reference tree and examination of the host-contig neighborhood, including
IdrB/Rieske and IdrP-like cytochrome-peroxidase genes and contig-edge status.
